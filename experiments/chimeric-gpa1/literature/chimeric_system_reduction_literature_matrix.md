## R1 Builder / Padding Extraction

2026-07-03 follow-up extraction focused on Route 1 (`tight-full-rebuild`) and used only source-backed statements for builder behavior.

- CHARMM-GUI Membrane Builder source extracted: S16 states a five-step workflow for membrane systems. A protein or protein complex is handled through PDB Reader & Manipulator, orientation is adjusted in Step 2, system size is determined in Step 3, lipids/solvent/ions are generated in Step 4, and topology plus equilibration/production inputs are generated in Step 5. For planar bilayers, the membrane XY area is determined from the protein cross-sectional area along the membrane normal and the area per lipid.
- GROMACS membrane/pressure-coupling source extracted: S11 supports the implementation constraint that semi-isotropic scaling can be useful for systems with interfaces and scales XY together while scaling Z independently. S17 records GROMACS membrane-simulation cautions, including that lipid-bilayer systems with proteins often require care and that waters introduced into membrane interstices can be a known setup problem.
- Route 1 support: these sources support a CHARMM-GUI-style complete rebuild that retains the full protein complex, membrane, solvent, ions, topology, equilibration inputs, production inputs, and semi-isotropic membrane pressure-coupling compatibility.
- Route 1 non-support: these sources do not provide a project-specific minimum XY margin or Z water thickness for the Ste2-Gpa1/chimeric-Galpha object. They do not justify claiming a reduced chctrl prototype as a `candidate_replacement` until an actual build, atom-count delta, smoke/benchmark, and physical sanity evidence exist.

## Initial Reading Memo

### Strongest current support

- **Direct chimeric Galpha MD precedent**: S01 supports that C-terminal/alpha5 swapped Galpha receptor complexes can be studied with multi-replicate, hundreds-of-ns MD, but it does not solve the compute-reduction question.
- **Closest GROMACS/CHARMM-family analogy**: S03 supports GPCR + engineered Galpha/Mini-Gs style systems with CHARMM-GUI, POPC, GROMACS, and long trajectories.
- **Ste2/Gpa1 structure and interface context**: S02 and S04 support why chctrl remains the correct positive-control biology/geometry anchor.
- **Restraint-method support**: S07 and S08 are candidate method sources for flat-bottom/contact restraint concepts. They still need method-section extraction before any force constant or schedule is proposed.
- **Tool behavior**: S09-S14 support implementation and analysis behavior only. They do not support biological pass/fail thresholds.

### No-go warnings

- No source in this first pass supports treating an extracted receptor-ICL/alpha5/eCT subsystem as equivalent to full GPCR-Galpha-in-membrane MD.
- No source in this first pass supports hard freezing (`freezegrps`) as a default membrane-production shortcut.
- No source in this first pass provides universal cutoffs for alpha5 iRMSD, BSA retention, contact retention, H-bond occupancy, COM distance, or junction RMSF. These remain project operational heuristics unless later source-audited.
- Coarse-grained/Martini evidence is not atomistic interface validation unless a later workflow includes backmapping and atomistic comparison.
