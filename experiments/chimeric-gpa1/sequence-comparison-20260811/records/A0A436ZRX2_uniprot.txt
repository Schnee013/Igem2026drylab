CC   -!- FUNCTION: Guanine nucleotide-binding proteins (G proteins) are involved
CC       as modulators or transducers in various transmembrane signaling systems
CC       (Probable). GasA is involved in the sensing of nematode ascaroside
CC       pheromones to positively regulate trap formation for nematode capture
CC       (PubMed:38877225). Ascarosides activate the gprC at the plasma membrane
CC       and together with the G protein alpha subunit gasA, reprograms the cell
CC       to initiate trap formation (PubMed:38877225). GprC and gasA also
CC       function in mitochondria where gprC-gasA signaling stimulates
CC       respiration in mitochondria at the hyphal tip after induction with
CC       nematodes (PubMed:38877225). {ECO:0000269|PubMed:38877225,
CC       ECO:0000305}.
CC   -!- COFACTOR:
CC       Name=Mg(2+); Xref=ChEBI:CHEBI:18420;
CC         Evidence={ECO:0000250|UniProtKB:P18064};
CC   -!- SUBUNIT: Interacts with the G protein-coupled receptor gprC at the
CC       cytoplasmic membrane and at mitochondria (PubMed:38877225). G proteins
CC       are composed of 3 units; alpha, beta and gamma. The alpha chain
CC       contains the guanine nucleotide binding site (Probable).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:38877225, ECO:0000305}.
CC   -!- SUBCELLULAR LOCATION: Cell membrane {ECO:0000269|PubMed:38877225};
CC       Peripheral membrane protein {ECO:0000305|PubMed:38877225}.
CC       Mitochondrion outer membrane {ECO:0000269|PubMed:38877225}; Peripheral
CC       membrane protein {ECO:0000305|PubMed:38877225}.
CC   -!- INDUCTION: Expression is up-regulated when the G protein-coupled
CC       receptor gprC is deleted. {ECO:0000269|PubMed:38877225}.
CC   -!- DISRUPTION PHENOTYPE: Results in complete loss of trap formation
CC       (PubMed:38877225). Impairs the increase in oxygen consumption by hyphae
CC       after induction with nematodes (PubMed:38877225). Leads to the
CC       induction of the G protein-coupled receptor gprC (PubMed:38877225).
CC       {ECO:0000269|PubMed:38877225}.
CC   -!- SIMILARITY: Belongs to the G-alpha family. G(q) subfamily.
CC       {ECO:0000305}.
