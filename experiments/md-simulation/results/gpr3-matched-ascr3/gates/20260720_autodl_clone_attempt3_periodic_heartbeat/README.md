## Outcome

The missing periodic heartbeat was an implementation gap in the temporary
attempt3 runtime wrapper: its loop sampled `nvidia-smi`, but never called the
formal event recorder. The production `mdrun` itself was healthy.

The source-first fix adds
`experiments/md-simulation/scripts/run_gpr_md_production_heartbeat.sh` and
routes newly generated `launch_command.sh` and `resume_command.sh` through its
supervisor. The runner binds to the exact PID, process-start identity, command,
TPR and deffnm; checks launch/source manifests, progress and output freshness,
checkpoint readability and checksum, and the exact fatal/LINCS/NaN/OOM/CUDA
issue set; then appends a locked event and atomically refreshes
`terminal_status.tsv`. A failed check records `UNRESOLVED` and grants no
authority to kill, restart, resume, or declare completion.

For the already-running attempt3, the runner was uploaded additively to:

```text
$RUN_ROOT/staging/heartbeat_attempt3_20260720T050121Z/
```

and attached with `--monitor-existing 11020` in:

```text
gpr3_r167y_ascr3_heartbeat_attempt3_20260720T044917Z
```

No frozen launch package, prior attempt, or production output was overwritten.
Before and after heartbeat-runner replacement, production remained the only
`gmx_mpi`, with PID `11020` and process-start identity `1851856070`.
