The right planning stance is therefore: use 2025 as the nearest iGEM-hosted proxy, keep 2021-2025 as a change-history baseline, and re-check once the 2026 [Medals](https://competition.igem.org/judging/medals) / [Special Prizes](https://competition.igem.org/judging/special-prizes) pages or 2026 Judge Handbook are posted.

### Practical Implications for This Project Design

The project design is a GPCR yeast biosensor for early field detection of plant-parasitic nematodes: ascaroside signal input, fungal Gpr receptors in *S. cerevisiae*, engineered STE2 pathway plus chimeric Gpa1, MAPK activation, colorimetric output, field visual readout, and a prevention-decision loop. The local project plan also separates sensing, pathway engineering, and social-impact/background workstreams.

Under the 2025 proxy, the provisional three-prize portfolio that best matches the design is:

1. Best Measurement: strong fit if the team produces repeatable wet-lab characterization of ascaroside quantification, receptor/pathway response, colorimetric output, calibration, controls, units, and error analysis.
2. Best Model: strong fit if docking, ODE/signaling, dose-response, or deployment models use wet-lab measurements and change design decisions.
3. Best Integrated Human Practices: strong fit if stakeholder, safety, farming, deployment, and ethics work changes project purpose, design, experiments, or field-use plans.

Best Sustainable Development Impact is a serious alternative to Best Integrated Human Practices if the strongest evidence becomes SDG stakeholder feedback, long-term agricultural impact analysis, deployment tradeoffs, and measurable sustainability contribution. Best Plant Synthetic Biology should not be chosen just because the application is agricultural; the 2025 text frames that prize around engineering plant, protoplast, algal, or other eukaryotic photosynthetic chassis work. Software and Part prizes should only be chosen if the project creates the needed open-source tool, Registry documentation, and experimental part evidence.
